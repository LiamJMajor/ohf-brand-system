# Pillars

The three beliefs everything we publish must serve. Stable, shared by every voice, and a filter: before anything ships, name the pillar it advances. If you cannot, it is not our story to tell.

Foundation comms embody the pillars directly. Project comms are pillars translated into product benefits, one `###` per pillar in `projects/<slug>/messaging.md`.

## Privacy
Your data is yours. Your home should not spy on you. A smart home must work locally: sharing data is a deliberate act, cloud services are optional and opt-in, and your behaviour at home is never shaped by an algorithm you did not choose.

## Choice
Your devices should work for you, together. Your home should not have a landlord. Devices from any maker, cooperating over open standards and local APIs, with no vendor lock-in and no arbitrary barriers to interoperability.

## Sustainability
Hardware should outlive its maker's interest in it. Your home should not end up in landfill. Devices must be durable, open to repurposing, and part of a system that helps you track and reduce waste and emissions.
