# Home Assistant: audiences

Key messages in messaging.md mirror these headings. Audiences drafted from who the site and docs address; objections supplied in interview 2026-09-14.

## People running it at home
**Cares about:** reliability, what breaks on update, new integrations for devices they own, energy, voice, dashboards.
**Found on:** the release blog, community forum, Discord, r/homeassistant, YouTube release party. (https://www.home-assistant.io/help/)
**Comes to us from:** the previous monthly release.
**Objections:** fear over stability and breakage when they update (interview 2026-09-14).

## People deciding to try it
**Cares about:** how hard setup is, whether it works with the devices they have, whether it is really private, what hardware to buy.
**Found on:** YouTube, Reddit, search, friends who run it.
**Comes to us from:** a vendor app or a cloud assistant ecosystem. (Intake fact, never a criticism.)
**Objections:** complexity of set-up; the learning curve (interview 2026-09-14).

## Contributors and integration developers
**Cares about:** architecture, how to add device support, review turnaround, quality scale.
**Found on:** GitHub, the developer portal, developer Discord channels.
**Comes to us from:** wanting their own device to work.
**Objections:** the overwhelming size of the project; lack of experience contributing; not knowing where to start or how to contribute (interview 2026-09-14).
