# Home Assistant: content

## Themes
Observed from the home page features and recent posts (https://www.home-assistant.io/, blog index). Confirm and rank.
- Local control and privacy ("All your smart home data stays local")

- Voice (Assist)
- Energy (Home Energy Management)
- Dashboards and automations
- Community and contributors (Community Day, contributor credits, open survey dataset)

- Monthly release post: long-form, one section per feature, screenshots, contributor credits, patch releases, backward-incompatible changes at the end.
- Feature shorts: TODO format (comms architecture T+1 per feature log item).
