# Rubric: on-brand

Score each axis 1 to 5. Below 3 on any axis fails. Used by every skill's self-review and by `evaluate-skill`.

| Axis | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Truth** | States a fact not in `truths/` | All facts traceable, one unclear | Every fact cites a truth path |
| **Voice** | Breaks two or more contrast pairs | Breaks one | Passes all pairs |
| **Mode** | Wrong energy for the channel | Close | Matches `modes.md` for the intake |
| **Audience** | Generic | Right audience, generic concerns | Speaks to that audience's stated concerns |
| **Editable** | Flattened or locked | Editable with effort | Requester can change anything in one pass |
| **Boundary** | Escalation trigger missed | Flagged late | Checked at intake and at output |
