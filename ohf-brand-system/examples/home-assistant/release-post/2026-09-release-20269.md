# Home Assistant 2026.9

## Context
Monthly stable release post, published on the first Wednesday of the month by Frenck. Medium impact by the comms architecture's definition: a .0 release with notable features. Candidate example proposed by onboard-project; awaiting the marketing team's confirmation.

## The work
Excerpt. Full post at the source URL.

Opening: "I'll be honest: Modbus has never been the flashiest part of Home Assistant. It quietly powers solar inverters, heat pumps, and energy meters, but only if you're comfortable hand-writing your own register map in YAML, a real wall that's kept a huge category of devices out of reach."

Structure: sixteen sections, one per feature area, then Integrations, Other noteworthy changes, Patch releases, Need help, Backward-incompatible changes. Features covered: Modbus, Cloud speech-to-text, Security dashboard alerts and favourites, History and Activity sources pane, Matter network map, tile card, sun automations, chart accessibility, Serial and MQTT panels, network storage.

## Why it works
- The opening admits the feature was never flashy, then explains the wall it removes. The reader knows why they should care before the feature is named.
- "From what changed to why it changed" is a section heading and a philosophy: the release is explained as what the person can now see and do.
- Handles such as @googanhiem, @piitaya and @Diegorro98 are credited next to the work, not in a list at the end.
- Breaking changes are structured and last, so a reader who scrolls for them finds them.
- One musical-note emoji, in the paragraph about hearing the shape of a chart. The playfulness matches the subject.

## What to copy
- Problem first, then the fix, in the first paragraph.
- One section per feature with a screenshot, sentence-case headings.
- Credit by handle inside the section.
- Fixed closing order: Integrations, Other noteworthy changes, Patch releases, Need help, Backward-incompatible changes.

## What not to copy
- The specific Modbus framing; it worked because Modbus really was neglected. Do not manufacture candour.
- Emoji beyond the fixed opening: the musical note here was earned by the subject. The opening "Home Assistant YYYY.M! 🎉" is house style; other emoji are the exception.
