## Origin
Home Assistant was started in 2013 by Paulus Schoutsen. By 2024 it had grown into an ecosystem of hundreds of projects, standards and libraries with no permanent home, and no structural protection against the thing that happens to successful open source: acquisition, then enshittification. The Open Home Foundation was created in 2024 as a tax-exempt non-profit *Stiftung* in Switzerland to own and govern that ecosystem, so it could not be bought. (https://www.openhomefoundation.org/about/, https://www.openhomefoundation.org/structure/)
