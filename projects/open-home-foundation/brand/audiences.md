# Open Home Foundation: audiences

One section per audience. Key messages in messaging.md mirror these headings. Drafted 2026-09-15 from `core/voices.md` ("the public, press, industry, contributors, funders") and from who the foundation's own posts actually address. The order below is a guess at priority; the interview sets the real one.

## People who live in a smart home and have never been offered an alternative
**Cares about:** their stuff working, and not being watched or upsold. The IFA post describes them as ranging from tech enthusiasts to people who just want reliable devices and have no awareness of the privacy question.
**Found on:** IFA and other public-facing events, mainstream press, social. Not in the forums yet.
**Comes to us from:** a bad experience: a device bricked after an acquisition, a feature moved behind a subscription, an app that stopped working.
**Objections:** sounds technical; sounds like it needs a project; I already bought into an ecosystem.

## Press and industry
**Cares about:** whether this is a real organisation with a real structure, and whether the criticism is substantiated.
**Found on:** trade press, IFA and CES coverage, regulatory consultations.
**Comes to us from:** a story about lock-in, a regulatory decision, or a competitor's misstep.
**Objections:** is this an advocacy front for Home Assistant's commercial interests? Who funds it?

## Regulators and policy people
**Cares about:** evidence, specificity, and whether an intervention would change anything.
**Found on:** European Commission consultations and equivalent processes.
**Comes to us from:** a call for evidence.
**Objections:** TODO. The Android interoperability post is the only public example; the interview should say whether this audience is real and served deliberately, or incidental.

## Contributors and the community
**Cares about:** that their work has a home that cannot be sold, and that showing up matters.
**Found on:** Discord, Reddit, Discourse, Community Day, GitHub.
**Comes to us from:** a project they already contribute to.
**Objections:** is the foundation overhead, or protection? Does governance change who decides?
