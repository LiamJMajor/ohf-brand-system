# Open Home Foundation: naming

Drafted 2026-09-15 from the foundation's own website and blog. Confirm in interview.

## Canonical
- Full name: Open Home Foundation
- Short name: "the foundation", lowercase, after a first full mention in the same piece. The website uses this throughout its own prose. TODO confirm.
- Capitalisation in a sentence: Open Home Foundation, always all three words capitalised. Never "open home foundation".
- "the open home" (lowercase) is the thing being fought for, not the organisation. The website uses both in one breath; the capital marks the difference. TODO confirm.

## Feature naming
The foundation does not ship features; its projects do. Initiatives and events it does name: Community Day, State of the Open Home, the Annual Report. Pattern: title case, no year in the name, year appended only where a specific edition is meant ("Community Day 2026"). TODO confirm and add a third example.

## Forbidden variants
| Do not write | Write instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| OHF | Open Home Foundation, then "the foundation" | Project and organisation names are never shortened (CLAUDE.md). The initialism appears in internal repo and channel names only; it has never appeared in published copy. TODO confirm |
| The Open Home Foundation (capital T mid-sentence) | the Open Home Foundation | "The" is not part of the registered name |
| Open Home | Open Home Foundation, or "the open home" if the cause is meant | "Open Home" alone reads as a product name and there is no such product |
| a product of / owned by the Open Home Foundation | a project of the Open Home Foundation | Projects are stewarded, not sold; "owned by" invites the acquisition framing the foundation exists to prevent. TODO confirm against the licence wording |

## Attribution
Projects attribute to the foundation, not the other way round. First mention in press or partner contexts: "Home Assistant, a project of the Open Home Foundation". When the foundation speaks about itself it needs no attribution line.
