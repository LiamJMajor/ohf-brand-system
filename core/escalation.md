# Escalation: what always needs a human

Skills check intake and draft against this table. When a trigger fires, the skill stops, marks the draft "needs review", and names the route. Triggers are observable from the intake or the draft, not from intent.

| Trigger (observable) | Route | Why |
|---|---|---|
| No pillar can be named for the output | do not ship | Not our story to tell (pillars.md) |
| A company, product or person is named critically | foundation leadership sign-off; evidence bar met | No solo call-outs, ever (voices.md, Criticism) |
| Foundation voice requested for a single project's news | check the four foundation-news criteria; if none met, route to the project | The foundation never speaks ahead of a project |
| Security disclosure, outage or community incident | project owner fronts; foundation amplifies only | Crisis rule (voices.md) |
| Foundation voice output contains a call to buy, a price, a store or product link, or a launch claim | split: the project promotes, the foundation may contextualise | Advocacy and selling never share a post (voices.md, Commercial boundary). Naming hardware is not selling: the foundation may say what it ran, built or tested on. The trigger is the ask, not the noun (interview 2026-09-15) |
| New product, feature or project name | core owner | Naming is identity work |
| Pricing, licensing, legal or partnership terms | leadership | Commitments |
| A proof point with Verified empty or "Never" | truths owner | Not usable until verified |
| Rubric self-score below 3 on any axis | requester's reviewer | Honest about reliability |

## Fallback
While waiting, the requester gets the draft clearly marked, plus the reviewer's name and expected turnaround.
