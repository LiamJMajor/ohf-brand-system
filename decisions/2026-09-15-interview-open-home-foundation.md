# Interview: Open Home Foundation

Interviewed @liam on 2026-09-15, following `onboard-project` stage 2 drafting from the foundation's website and blog. Prompted by a real request — the social post set for the IFA 2026 blog — which could not be built because the project was `registered` with every file a stub.

## The four rules
All four candidates drafted from published work were confirmed unchanged. Each keeps its real Yes and constructed No in `brand/voice.md`:

- **Name the company and the conduct.** The Android interoperability post is the evidence: "Google gatekept key Android features." Vague criticism is toothless.
- **Second person, and what you get to do.** The pillars are written this way on every surface where the foundation speaks first.
- **Show us doing it, not believing it.** Credibility here is behavioural. The Plausible analytics post is the pattern: we did the thing and published how.
- **Warm, not corporate, at the door.** "Pull up a chair" over "is pleased to announce".

Four for four is worth noting, and worth being slightly suspicious of. The rules were drafted from the same body of published work they were then checked against, so agreement partly measures the drafting rather than the judgment. The test is the next piece written to them by someone who was not in the room.

## Modes: set-piece versus steady state
Rejected alternatives were audience ("who's listening") and stakes ("what's at stake"). The reason the third framing won matters: the foundation's register does not soften as the stakes rise. A named call-out reads the same in a forum reply as in the Annual Report. What changes between Low, Medium and High is how much is built around the argument, not how carefully it is phrased.

That is an unusual property and it is now written down, because the obvious default — get more formal when the audience gets more important — is exactly wrong here and an agent would reach for it.

## Fourth personality attribute: playful with the format
The three drafted attributes were all credibility claims. The missing one is that the foundation makes its argument through form: a booth built as a living room, a cross-stitch on a trade show wall, a CD wall with physical tags.

Deliberately distinguished from *Hospitable, not institutional*, which the draft had resting on the same IFA example. Hospitable is how people are received; playful is what the foundation will do to a format to land a point. The booth did both, which is why it worked.

## Codify queue
Blog post → social post set first, then partner announcement. Both to `codify-workflow`. Named call-out and event write-up were considered and not prioritised, though the call-out is the highest-risk output the foundation makes and its obligations are already checkable in `core/escalation.md`.

## What this interview did not settle
Messaging. Boilerplate, key messages and taglines remain drafts, and no proof point is verified, so nothing in `brand/messaging.md` is usable by a skill. The interview protocol expects messaging to be walked top down; that round did not happen and is the next session's work.
