# Social register, and the tells that gave the drafts away

## How this came up
A social post set drafted for the IFA 2026 blog was rejected on three counts. Correcting them surfaced a fourth problem, which is that the brand system had no social examples in it at all, so the drafts were being written from blog evidence and channel classes.

Three approved posts were supplied and are now filed in `examples/open-home-foundation/social-post/`. They should have been harvested before anything was drafted.

## What was wrong

**Unexplained references.** The drafts said "IFA Berlin" with no gloss. `brand/audiences.md` puts people who have never been offered an alternative to a surveilled smart home first, and locates them at public events and in mainstream press. Those are exactly the people who do not know what IFA is. Now a rule in `voice.md`: gloss every event, acronym, standard, product and partner on first mention. The blog already did this; social had been skipping it.

**Em dashes.** Banned outright, every register, every surface. Recorded with unusual emphasis in `style.md` because an agent reaches for one about every second paragraph and will reintroduce them on any future pass unless the rule is loud.

**AI writing tells.** "It's not X, it's Y" and its variants, the standalone summary sentence ("Both halves are the argument"), escalating triads, and throat-clearing openers. The rejected drafts contained at least three of the four. They are now a table in `style.md` with the reason each fails, because "avoid AI tropes" is not checkable and a named list is.

## What the examples taught that nobody had to say
- Every approved post carries emoji: one in the opening line carrying the mood, and a 👇 at the link. The drafts had none. `style.md` previously recorded "no emoji observed" — drawn from blog copy, and wrong for social, which is a different surface.
- The shape is consistent: news in one line, then what is behind the link in concrete terms, then the link alone on the last line.
- The same copy runs across networks. The rejected drafts offered per-network variants, which is more work producing something further from house practice. Now recorded in the Modes table.
- Ampersands in social, spelled out in prose. Months abbreviated with the year dropped when obvious ("Sept 17").
- None of the three posts contains a sentence about what the foundation values. They show the thing and let it argue, which is the existing "Show us doing it" rule holding up under evidence nobody had collected yet.

## Modes gains a social row
`Medium` previously covered blog and social together. It does not: social is lighter and more playful than the blog it points at, and runs to 250–300 characters. Split into `Medium` and `Medium, social`.

## Worth saying plainly
Four of four voice rules were confirmed unchanged at the interview this morning, and this afternoon the first real output written to them was rejected on three counts. The rules were fine. What was missing was the register and the mechanics, and the examples that would have shown both. `harvest-examples` before drafting, not after.
