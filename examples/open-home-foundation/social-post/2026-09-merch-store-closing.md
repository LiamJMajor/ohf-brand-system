# Merch store closing (temporarily)

## Context
Social post about a store outage during a warehouse move. Supplied by @liam on 2026-09-15. Approximate date: posted shortly before 2026-09-17, which the copy names.

Worth noting for the escalation rules: this is a store post carrying a buy nudge. Under the commercial boundary it belongs to whatever brand is on the item, in store register, not foundation advocacy register (`core/voices.md`, Commercial boundary). It is filed here as a style reference, not as a licence to sell in advocacy voice.

## The work
> We're closing our merch store‼️
> (temporarily)
>
> We're moving warehouses, and improving our systems. So the store's going to be unavailable for about two weeks, starting Sept 17.
>
> It would be very helpful if we had less stock to move between warehouses 👀 *cough*.
>
> https://store.openhomefoundation.org

## Why it works
- The opening is deliberately alarming and the correction lands on the next line, in parentheses, at a lower volume. The joke is the line break.
- Three facts, no padding: why, how long, from when. "Sept 17" abbreviated, no year.
- The sales ask is the last thing and it never asks. "👀 *cough*" does it, and the reader is in on it.
- Contractions throughout ("We're", "store's"), which is what keeps a logistics announcement readable.

## What to copy
- Let a line break carry a joke rather than explaining it.
- Practical facts in plain order: why, how long, from when.
- Asides in parentheses or as stage directions when the ask would otherwise read as marketing.
- Month abbreviated ("Sept 17"), year omitted when it is obvious.

## What not to copy
- The buy nudge, in foundation advocacy voice. It works here because this is store register.
- Do not manufacture the self-deprecation. It works because the inconvenience is genuinely the foundation's own fault.
